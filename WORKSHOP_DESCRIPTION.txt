[h1]Morghasts & Dread Abyssals for Vampire Counts[/h1]

Unlocks the Nagash DLC Morghasts and the Dread Abyssal mounts for the whole Vampire Counts race — no need to play the Nagash campaign to field them.

[h2]Morghasts for every Vampire Counts faction[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Morghast Harbingers[/b] — recruitable from the Haunted Wood chain, tier 4+
[*][b]Morghast Archai[/b] — recruitable from the Haunted Wood chain, tier 5
[*]Building-based unit caps (Tomb Kings style): T4 gives 1 Harbinger, T5 gives 2 Harbingers + 1 Archai; multiple provinces stack
[*]The ghost-branch technologies grant +1 Harbinger (Legacy of the Tombs) and +1 Archai (Nightmare Spirits)
[*]Vanilla hire costs, upkeep and stats
[/list]

[h2]Dread Abyssal mounts[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Mannfred[/b] gets Ashigaroth, Devourer of the Craven in every campaign
[*][b]Neferata[/b] gets her Dread Abyssal in every campaign
[*]The mount skill nodes are CA's own — the mod only removes the "Nagash campaign only" gate; level requirements stay vanilla
[/list]

[h2]Skill & tech buffs extended to Morghasts[/h2]
[list]
[*]Raising the Dead (Black Knights / Blood Knights / Drakenhof Templars lord skill)
[*]The flying-monsters lord skill (Fell Bats / Vargheists / Terrorgheists / Zombie Dragons, rank 7+)
[*]Ghost-branch tech buffs (battle healing cap; barrier line is listed but Morghasts have no barrier)
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Requires the Nagash DLC to see the units
[*]Save-game compatible: add or remove at any time
[*]Immortal Empires and all campaigns; the Nagash campaign itself is unaffected
[*]The cap tooltip line is English-only in all game languages
[/list]
